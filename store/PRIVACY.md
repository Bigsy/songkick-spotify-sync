# Privacy policy: Spotify to Songkick

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Spotify to Songkick is a browser extension that adds the artists you follow on Spotify to your Songkick tracked artists. It works entirely inside your own browser.

## What it reads

- **Spotify:** the names and Spotify IDs of the artists in your Your Library view, read from the Spotify tab you have open.
- **Songkick:** your tracked-artist list, Songkick search results, and artist pages, read from the Songkick tab you have open.

## What it stores

The artist lists, search results, your match choices and a history of additions are saved with Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. They are not synced to other devices. **Clear local data** on the sync page deletes all of it.

## What it sends, and to whom

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension has no server, analytics, advertising or tracking. The only network requests it makes are to `www.songkick.com`, from your Songkick tab, using your existing session: reading pages, and submitting Songkick's own Track form for artists you add. It makes no requests to Spotify beyond what the Spotify page itself does.

## What it never does

- It never reads, stores or transmits passwords, cookies or session tokens.
- It never unfollows artists on Spotify or untracks artists on Songkick.
- It never sells or shares data, and uses it for nothing other than matching and adding artists.
